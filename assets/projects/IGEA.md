Integrating Geosciences and Engineering in the Arctic (IGEA) was a unique class taught in three parts by Colin Gleason at UMass Amherst that I was fortunate to help with as a PhD student. In the Fall, undergraduate students learn how to plan and prepare for field science studying Arctic Hydrology. The students then follow through with their plans at Toolik Field Station on the North Slope of Alaska that Summer with the help of me and another graduate student. Finally, the students analyze their data and write a report in the Spring. The science objectives were broadly defined in the NSF CAREER grant to Gleason which gives the students lots of freedom to follow their interests.
