SWOT launched in December 2022, but in the decade preceding the launch there was a lot of research to establish the best methods to collect on-the-ground measurements to validate SWOT measurements. Namely, quantifying water surface elevations down to centimeter scale and surface water extent to a few meters. I have participated in and led several field campaigns starting with the Summer of 2017 in Canada and Alaska.
